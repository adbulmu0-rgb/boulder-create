# Boulder Create

Boulder Create is a decentralized creator economy platform that revolutionizes how creative works generate and distribute revenue through automated micro-royalty streams and collaborative IP ownership models. The platform introduces a novel "Creative DNA" system where each piece of content is tokenized with embedded metadata tracking its creative lineage, including original creators, collaborators, derivative works, and usage rights. Smart contracts automatically distribute payments in real-time based on predetermined contribution percentages when content generates revenue through streaming, licensing, remixes, or commercial use.

The platform employs zero-knowledge proofs for copyright verification without revealing sensitive creative processes and utilizes IPFS for decentralized storage with content fingerprinting for piracy detection. A unique "Inspiration Graph" maps relationships between works, ensuring derivative creators properly compensate original artists even across complex creative chains. Smart contracts handle escrow for commissioned works, automated audit trails for rights management, and cross-platform royalty collection from external services through API integrations.

Boulder Create enables fractional ownership of creative works, allowing fans and investors to purchase stakes in songs, artworks, or other IP while creators retain creative control. The platform includes a built-in marketplace for licensing creative assets, collaboration tools for multi-creator projects, and governance mechanisms allowing the creative community to vote on platform policies. Creators can set flexible monetization models including pay-per-use, subscription tiers, and dynamic pricing based on popularity metrics.
